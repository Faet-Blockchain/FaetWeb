window.ethereum.request({ method: "eth_chainId" }) is returning the correct value,
but you're reading it before MetaMask has fully updated — particularly right after a user switches networks or connects the wallet.

Always use a fresh read from MetaMask inside checkNetwork() rather than relying on event-provided chainId.

Move the "current chain ID" comparison logic into a reliable helper that is called both on connection and event triggers.
